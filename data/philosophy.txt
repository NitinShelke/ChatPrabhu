Title: Core Philosophies of Kṛṣṇa Consciousness

Content:
ISKCON's philosophy is based on the Vedic scriptures, especially the Bhagavad-gītā and Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, as explained by Śrīla Prabhupāda. Key tenets include:

We are not the body; we are eternal souls (ātmā).

Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Personality of Godhead (Bhagavān).

The goal of life is to awaken love for God (prema-bhakti).

Chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahāmantra purifies the heart:
Hare Kṛṣṇa, Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa, Hare Hare, Hare Rāma, Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma, Hare Hare

Devotional service (bhakti-yoga) is the means of attaining liberation and Kṛṣṇa’s eternal abode.

Four regulative principles: No meat-eating, no intoxication, no gambling, no illicit sex.

Surrender to a bona fide spiritual master is essential for spiritual advancement.
