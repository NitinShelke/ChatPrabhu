Title: The Disciplic Succession (Paramparā)

Content:
ISKCON belongs to the Brahma-Madhva-Gauḍīya Sampradāya, a disciplic succession that traces spiritual knowledge from Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa down through His pure devotees.

The paramparā includes:

Śrī Kṛṣṇa

Lord Brahmā – The first living entity created by Kṛṣṇa.

Nārada Muni – The traveling sage and son of Brahmā.

Vyāsadeva – The compiler of the Vedas, Mahābhārata, and Bhāgavatam.

Madhvācārya – The founder of the Dvaita (dualistic) school of Vedānta.

Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu – Kṛṣṇa Himself in a devotee's form, who spread the chanting of the holy names.

The Six Gosvāmīs of Vṛndāvana – Rūpa, Sanātana, Jīva, and others who wrote foundational Vaiṣṇava texts.

Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura, Gaurakiśora dāsa Bābājī, Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura, and Śrīla Prabhupāda.

This unbroken chain ensures that spiritual knowledge remains pure and unchanged
