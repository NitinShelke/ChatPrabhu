Title: Great Ācāryas of the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Tradition

Content:
The Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava tradition has been upheld and expanded by great spiritual masters. A few notable ones include:

Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu (1486–1534): Kṛṣṇa in the form of a devotee, who inaugurated the saṅkīrtana movement and propagated the chanting of the holy names.

Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī: Defined the science of bhakti-rasa and wrote Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu.

Śrīla Sanātana Gosvāmī: Authored Hari-bhakti-vilāsa and served as a leading theologian.

Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī: Nephew of Rūpa and Sanātana, he wrote Ṣaṭ-sandarbhas, the philosophical cornerstone of Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism.

Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura (1838–1914): A visionary preacher and writer who brought back the authenticity of Lord Caitanya’s teachings.

Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura (1874–1937): Established 64 maṭhas and emphasized purity, preaching, and printing.

Śrīla Prabhupāda: Founder-ācārya of ISKCON who took the movement worldwide.
